      Common questions

      How is the rental price calculated?

      Each item has a price per day. Your estimated total is the kit rate per day multiplied by the number of days between your pickup and return dates.

      What happens if gear is damaged?

      Normal wear is fine. For serious damage, a part of the deposit can be kept to cover repair. This is a student demo, so these terms are examples.

      Can I rent for just one night?

      Yes. Choose a return date one day after pickup and the estimate shows a one-day rental.

      Is this a real booking website?

      No. TrailNest is a CSE326 project. Bookings are saved only in your own browser and no payment or reservation is made.
